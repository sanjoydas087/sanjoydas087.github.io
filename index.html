HHS care load forecasting

Predicting care load and placement demand

  • Python
  • Streamlit
  • Scikit-learn
  • Statsmodels
  • Plotly
  • Five-module dashboard on 1,061 days of data, with early-warning alerts and what-if scenarios.
  • Benchmarked seven models: Naive, Moving Average, Holt-Winters, ARIMA, SARIMA, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting.
  • Random Forest scored RMSE 6.6 on a 60-day holdout. I also ran 5-fold rolling-origin cross-validation and a 40-origin walk-forward backtest, which showed that long-horizon error grows faster than the square root of the horizon.
  • Found a structural break: average daily care load fell about 71% from 2023 to 2025. Wrote a research paper and an executive summary.
